## Slide-by-slide checklist

### 1. Three service types
- Soundbite: "SaaS: use it. PaaS: build on it. IaaS: rent the machine."
- Cover: SaaS: the provider runs a finished app like Office 365 or Google Docs; you tweak settings only. PaaS: you build and deploy your own app on the provider's tools, like Azure with .NET and Java, without managing infrastructure. IaaS: you rent raw resources, like an AWS server with Linux, Apache, 16 GB RAM and 160 GB storage. Say: SaaS, use it. PaaS, build on it. IaaS, rent the machine.
- Source: 09 Handout p.1

### 2. Who manages what
- Soundbite: "Each step down, you manage more."
- Cover: Our own summary of the handout definitions, not a handout figure. SaaS: the provider runs almost everything. PaaS: you own your app and data; the provider runs the platform and hardware. IaaS: the provider gives you machines; you manage what runs on them. Say: Each step down, you manage more.
- Source: Our summary of 09 Handout p.1 definitions (inferred)

### 3. 10 benefits of cloud computing
- Soundbite: "Scale up fast. Pay for what you use."
- Cover: Ten benefits, in handout order: instant scalability, anywhere accessibility, speed and agility, reduced expenditure, automatic updates, disaster recovery, high security, reduced carbon footprint, flexibility, enterprise collaboration. One honest caveat from the handout: cost depends on consumption, a variable expense, so the cloud is not automatically cheaper. Say: Scale up fast. Pay for what you use.
- Source: 09 Handout p.1-2
