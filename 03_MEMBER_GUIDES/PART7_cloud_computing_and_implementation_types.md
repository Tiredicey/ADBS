## Slide-by-slide checklist

### 1. What cloud computing is
- Soundbite: "If it has the web, it has your data."
- Cover: Handout definition: services delivered over the Internet, including storage, servers, databases, networking and software. Files live in a remote database instead of a local drive, so any device with web access reaches them. Say: If it has the web, it has your data.
- Source: 09 Handout p.1

### 2. Three implementation types
- Soundbite: "Rent it, own it, or mix both."
- Cover: Defined by where the infrastructure lives and who controls it. Public: third party, sold to everyone; AWS, Google App Engine, Microsoft Azure. Reliable and scalable but not for organizations under strict data-security rules. Private: one organization, for itself. Hybrid: both, for example email on public and financials on private. Say: Rent it, own it, or mix both.
- Source: 09 Handout p.1

### 3. Our class example: MongoDB Atlas
- Soundbite: "Our database, their data center."
- Cover: Our own lab is an example. The handout creates a free shared cluster on AWS or Google Cloud in Singapore, then connects from Compass with a mongodb+srv string. Because Atlas runs on a public provider, our class database is public cloud. That last line is our inference from the handout, not a quote. Say: Our database, their data center.
- Source: 09 Handout p.2-3 (last bullet is our inference)
